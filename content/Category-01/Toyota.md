## GR Corolla Circuit Edition
### $40,000

In the middle of the pack, and the one I feel you get the most bang for your buck, is the Toyota GR Corolla. The GRC is unique as it was a limited production car with 1,500 units sold in the US in 2023. 

> New 2024 Circuit Edition models start around $46,235 
> All other models range between $36,000 and $44,500

This price range includes 
- 1.6L turbocharged 3-cylinder and 
- 300 hp @ 6,500 rpm and 273 lb-ft of tourqe @ 3,000 rpm
- 6-speed manual with rev-matching (iMT)
- GR-FOUR All-Wheel Drive with selectable torque splits (60:40, 50:50, or 30:70)
- 21 mpg city / 28 mpg highway / 24 mpg combined
- 18-inch forged wheels (such as BBS forged wheels on 2024 models) with 235/40R18 tires
- Ventilated rotors with 4-piston front and 2-piston rear calipers
- Forged carbon fiber roof, vented hood bulge, larger rear spoiler, and front parking sensors (on 2024 models)
- Sport seats with synthetic leather/suede accents and blue stitching, heated front seats, heated steering wheel, GR shift knob, 8-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and an 8-speaker JBL sound system
