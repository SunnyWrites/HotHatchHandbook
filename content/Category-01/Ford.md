## Focus RS

### $27,000

No longer in production, The Focus RS comes in on the lower end of the price scale. This hot hatch was in production between 2016–2018 in the U.S. and has become a rare, collectors item in the hatch world. Like the GR Corolla, the 2028 model was a limited run production of only 1,000 units. 

> Used models prices generally average around $26,000 to $27,750 with some retail brand tipping the scale just above $31,000 

This price includes: 
- 2.3L DOHC 16-valve turbocharged inline-4 with direct injection
- 350 hp @ 6,000 rpm / 350 lb-ft @ 3,200 rpm
- 6-speed manual
- All-wheel drive with rear torque-vectoring and Normal, Sport, Track, and Drift modes
- 19 city / 25 highway / 22 combined mpg
- Brembo brakes; 19-inch alloy/forged wheels standard (18-inch wheels available/optional depending on market or package)
- 390-watt Sony 10-speaker audio system including a cargo-area subwoofer) paired with an 8-inch SYNC 3 touchscreen
- 8-inch LCD color touchscreen running Ford SYNC 3, featuring Apple CarPlay, Android Auto, Bluetooth streaming, SiriusXM satellite radio, HD Radio, and two USB ports
