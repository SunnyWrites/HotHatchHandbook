## Civic Type R
### $45,000

The Civic Type R is another hot hatch the remains in production. It comes in at the second highest in price on our list. 

>New models average between **$48,000 - $50,000**
>Older models range anywhere between **$30,000 - $47,000**

This price range includes:
- 315-hp 2.0-liter turbocharged inline 4-cylinder engine
- 6-speed manual transmission
- Front-wheel drive
- 4 drive modes (Comfort, Sport, +R, and Individual)
- 22 mpg city and 28 mpg highway
- Brembo 4-piston aluminum front calipers with 13.8-inch rotors
- 19 x 9.5J matte black wheels with 265/30ZR19 Michelin Pilot Sport 4S summer tires
- 9-inch touchscreen, 10.2-inch digital instrument cluster, Google built-in with Gemini, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Honda LogR datalogger, and a 12-speaker Bose sound system
