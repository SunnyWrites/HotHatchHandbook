## Golf R
### $50,000

The VW Golf R comes in as our priciest option at an average of $50,000. 

> The Golf R is still in production with a new 2027 ranging from **$49,465 to $58,905** MSRP depending on trim level.
> Models from 2022 - 2024 can range from **$44,090–$46,465** original MSRP

This price includes: 
- 328-hp 2.0-liter turbocharged engine
- 7-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission
- R-Performance 4MOTION torque vectoring
- DCC adaptive chassis control
- 22 mpg city / 31 mpg highway / 25 mpg combined (14.5-gallon tank)
- IQ.DRIVE suite including adaptive cruise/Travel Assist features, blind-spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert, and lane-keep assist
- 12.9-inch infotainment screen, Harman Kardon audio, Apple CarPlay/Android Auto
- 19-inch forged wheels with performance tires, blue "R" brake calipers, quad-tipped exhaust, rear spoiler/diffuser, front light bar, and illuminated VW badge
- Contoured sport seats with blue accents, heated flat-bottom steering wheel, multi-zone climate control, and heated outboard rear seats
